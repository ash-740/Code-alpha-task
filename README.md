✅ PHISHING AWARENESS TRAINING MODULE


---

🎯 1. Introduction to Phishing

What is phishing?
A type of cyberattack where attackers trick people into revealing personal or sensitive information using fake emails, messages, or websites.

Common Targets:

Emails

SMS (smishing)

Voice calls (vishing)

Fake login pages




---

⚠️ 2. Real-World Phishing Examples

Email impersonating a bank: "Verify your account to avoid suspension."

Fake Google login: Exact replica, but URL is different.

COVID-19 scams: "Get vaccine registration here."


> ✅ Example Activity: Show 2 emails—ask users to identify the real vs fake.




---

🕵️ 3. How to Recognize a Phishing Attempt

Red Flags in Emails:

Generic greetings (“Dear user”)

Spelling/grammar mistakes

Urgent or threatening tone (“Act now or lose access”)

Suspicious links (hover before clicking)

Unusual sender email (e.g., admin@paypal-notice.xyz)


Fake Websites:

Slightly misspelled domain names (e.g., amaz0n.com)

No HTTPS or a strange certificate

Poor design or outdated look




---

🧠 4. Social Engineering Tactics

Pretending to be someone you trust (boss, IT admin)

Creating urgency/fear to rush decisions

Offering fake rewards or prizes



---

🛡️ 5. Best Practices to Stay Safe

Check the sender’s email address carefully

Never click unknown links or download suspicious attachments

Verify requests by contacting the sender through other means

Use multi-factor authentication (MFA)

Update passwords regularly

Install antivirus and anti-phishing tools



---

📚 6. Interactive Quiz (Sample Questions)

Q1: What is a common sign of a phishing email?
a) It uses your name
b) It has an urgent call to action
c) It is from a known contact
✅ Correct answer: b

Q2: You receive an email asking you to reset your password but the link leads to goog1e.com. What should you do?
✅ Correct answer: Don’t click the link, report it as phishing.

Q3: What does HTTPS in a URL indicate?
✅ Answer: The site uses encryption (SSL). It’s more secure.


---

🧾 7. Summary Slide

Phishing is dangerous but avoidable

Look for red flags

Don’t trust links or attachments blindly

Verify before you act

Stay aware and alert
